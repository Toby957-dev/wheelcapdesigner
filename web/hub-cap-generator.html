How it works

  1. Enter measurements. The outer diameter and the clamp/mounting diameter of the wheel opening. Tip: measure on the wheel — the clamp Ø drives the fit.
  2. Pick a logo. A built-in shape (star, hexagon, rings …), text, or your own SVG. Place, rotate, scale it — raised, engraved or flush two-color.
  3. Check in 3D. Rotate the model live, set cap and logo colors, verify the clips and the fit.
  4. Download & print. As an STL (single color) or a 3MF with separate, colored objects (perfect for multi-color / AMS). Ready for Bambu Studio, PrusaSlicer & co.

FAQ

Is the hub cap generator really free?

Yes. WheelCapDesigner runs free in your browser — no signup, no installation.

Which measurements do I need?

Two: the visible outer diameter and the clamp/mounting diameter of the wheel opening. Measure on the wheel — the clamp Ø is what determines the fit.

STL or 3MF — which one?

STL for single-color prints. The 3MF contains cap and logo as separate, pre-colored objects — ideal for multi-color / AMS, no manual painting.

Will the printed cap stay in the wheel?

Yes, thanks to adjustable snap-in clips. If it's too tight or loose, just tweak the tolerance for your printer.

Can I use my own logo?

Absolutely — upload an SVG or use built-in shapes and text, optionally flush two-color for top-down printing.

Which material should I use?

For outdoor use, ASA or PETG hold up better than PLA (UV/heat). Darker colors age more slowly.
